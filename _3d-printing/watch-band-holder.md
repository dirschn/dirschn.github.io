Have you ever had that annoying moment when the little loop that keeps your watch band tidy breaks? It happened to me! The small rubber holder (I'm not sure what the official name is either) on my watch band ripped unexpectedly, leaving the excess band flapping around.

Rather than buying an entirely new band, I decided to design and 3D print a replacement. Using flexible TPU filament, I created this simple but effective watch band holder that slips right over the band. The finished piece fit perfectly and kept my watch band neat and secure.

It served me well for months until eventually the rest of the band wore out and I had to replace the whole thing. But this quick fix definitely extended the life of my original band!
