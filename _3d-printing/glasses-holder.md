We recently moved into a new apartment and while we were settling in I realized how nice it would be to have a designated spot to put my glasses. I looked through Thingiverse but couldn't find many glasses holders that go on the wall, and only one that I actually liked the look of. I tried that one out, but it ended up being too small for my glasses, so to FreeCAD I went!

It's a really simple design, mostly because I'm at whatever level is below absolute beginner with 3D modeling, but I didn't really want anything large and gaudy anyway.
